The Economics of Urban Beekeeping

For most of the twentieth century, commercial apiaries were pushed steadily outward from city limits. Municipal by-laws framed hives as a nuisance rather than an asset, and the assumption went largely unchallenged until yield data from rooftop colonies began to contradict it.

Rooftop colonies in three European capitals produced, on average, 18% more honey per hive than their rural counterparts — a result researchers attribute to the sheer variety of flowering species in parks and private gardens.

Before you ask

The five questions every director asks.

If yours isn't here, contact us directly and we'll answer it live.

How fast is onboarding?
Most institutes run their first live mock within 48 hours. Set up your test parameters and pick a test date — we handle the rest. Enterprise accounts get a dedicated onboarding manager who sets up branches and roles with you on a call.
Is student data secure?
Data is encrypted in transit and at rest, hosted in-region, and access is scoped by role so a branch coordinator only ever sees their own centre. You can export or permanently delete a student record at any time, and archived students stop counting toward your plan while their history stays intact.
Can I use my own institute logo?
Yes — on the student login screen, the test interface header and every PDF band report. Custom domain options are also available so students never see a BandUltra URL.
What if my centre loses internet mid-test?
Answers are written locally as the candidate works and sync the moment the connection returns. The timer pauses on disconnect and the invigilator can resume the session from the exact question the student was on.
Do we have to switch our whole institute at once?
No. Most institutes start with one batch on the trial, run a parallel mock against their existing process, and move the rest once faculty are comfortable. Nothing about your current paper workflow has to change on day one.
