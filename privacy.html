1. Introduction

BandUltra ("BandUltra," "we," "us," or "our") provides a hybrid testing infrastructure platform that enables educational institutes, coaching centers, and test preparation providers ("Institutes," "Customers," or "you") to deliver IELTS/PTE-style practice tests, mock assessments, and related testing services to their students and candidates ("Test-Takers" or "End Users").

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard information when Institutes and their authorized users (administrators, instructors, and Test-Takers) use the BandUltra platform, including our web application, APIs, and any related services (collectively, the "Services").

This Policy applies to two categories of individuals whose data we may process, described further in Section 3:

  • Institute Personnel — administrators, teachers, and staff of an Institute who access BandUltra to configure and manage testing programs.
  • Test-Takers — students or candidates who take practice tests through the Services, typically invited or enrolled by an Institute.

If you are a Test-Taker, your Institute is generally responsible for your enrollment and for explaining how your data will be used in connection with your test preparation. Please also refer to Section 4 (Our Role as Data Processor/Controller) for more detail on this relationship.

By accessing or using the Services, you agree to the collection and use of information as described in this Policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the Services.

2. Scope

This Policy applies to information collected through the Services. It does not apply to third-party websites, applications, or services that may be linked to or integrated with the Services, which are governed by their own privacy policies.

Where an Institute has entered into a separate Data Processing Agreement ("DPA") with BandUltra, the terms of that DPA govern the processing of personal data submitted by or on behalf of the Institute (including Test-Taker data) to the extent of any conflict with this Policy.

3. Information We Collect

3.1 Information Provided by Institutes

When an Institute signs up for and administers the Services, we collect:

  • Account and business information: institute name, business address, tax/registration identifiers, billing contact, and payment details.
  • Institute personnel information: names, work email addresses, phone numbers, job titles, and login credentials for administrators, teachers, and staff.
  • Configuration data: test schedules, class/cohort structures, branding assets, and platform settings.

3.2 Information Collected from Test-Takers

To deliver hybrid IELTS/PTE testing functionality, the Services may collect the following categories of information about Test-Takers, generally provided by the Institute or directly by the Test-Taker during registration or test-taking:

  • Identity and contact information: full name, email address, phone number, date of birth, and, where required for identity verification, a government-issued ID or passport number/photo.
  • Enrollment data: Institute/class affiliation, enrollment dates, and candidate ID numbers.
  • Test performance data: responses to test items, scores, band predictions, timing data, and progress history across Listening, Reading, Writing, and Speaking modules.
  • Audio and, where applicable, video recordings: recordings of spoken responses for the Speaking module, and webcam/screen data where proctoring or identity-verification features are enabled by the Institute.
  • Device and usage data: IP address, browser type, operating system, device identifiers, and log data generated while using the Services (including for exam-integrity and anti-cheating monitoring, where enabled).

We collect only the categories of Test-Taker data that an Institute chooses to enable for its testing programs (for example, proctoring or ID verification are optional features that Institutes may turn on or off).

3.3 Information Collected Automatically

Across all users of the Services, we automatically collect certain technical information, including:

  • Log data (access times, pages viewed, actions taken, referring URLs).
  • Device and connection information (IP address, browser and OS version, device type).
  • Cookies and similar tracking technologies, as described in Section 9.

3.4 Information from Third Parties

We may receive information from third-party services integrated with the platform, such as single sign-on (SSO) providers, payment processors, and cloud infrastructure or proctoring partners engaged by us or by an Institute.

4. Our Role as Data Processor/Controller

BandUltra operates a B2B model. In most cases:

  • With respect to Institute account and billing data, BandUltra acts as a data controller (or equivalent) — we determine how this information is used to operate and bill for the Services.
  • With respect to Test-Taker data, BandUltra generally acts as a data processor (or "service provider," depending on applicable law) on behalf of the Institute, which acts as the data controller. The Institute is responsible for establishing a lawful basis for collecting and sharing Test-Taker data with us, and for responding to Test-Taker requests regarding their data, except where a separate DPA specifies otherwise.

Test-Takers with questions about how their data is used should, in the first instance, contact their Institute. BandUltra will support Institutes in fulfilling their obligations to Test-Takers as described in our DPA and Section 8 below.

5. How We Use Information

We use the information described above to:

  • Provide, operate, and maintain the Services, including delivering practice tests, scoring, analytics, and reporting to Institutes.
  • Authenticate users and manage accounts, roles, and permissions.
  • Process payments and manage billing relationships with Institutes.
  • Verify Test-Taker identity and monitor test integrity, where these features are enabled by an Institute.
  • Generate aggregated or de-identified analytics to help Institutes track cohort performance and to improve our scoring models and Services.
  • Communicate with Institute personnel and Test-Takers about the Services, including service updates, security notices, and support requests.
  • Detect, prevent, and address fraud, abuse, security incidents, and technical issues.
  • Comply with legal obligations and enforce our Terms of Service.
  • With separate, specific consent or as otherwise permitted by our agreement with an Institute, improve our automated scoring and assessment models.

We do not sell personal data, and we do not use Test-Taker data for third-party advertising purposes.

6. Legal Basis for Processing (EEA/UK Users)

Where applicable data protection law (such as the GDPR or UK GDPR) requires a legal basis for processing, we rely on one or more of the following:

  • Performance of a contract — to provide the Services under our agreement with an Institute.
  • Legitimate interests — to secure our platform, prevent fraud, and improve our Services, balanced against individual rights.
  • Legal obligation — to comply with applicable law, regulation, or legal process.
  • Consent — where required, such as for certain proctoring features, biometric-adjacent identity verification, or optional communications; consent can be withdrawn at any time.

7. How We Share Information

We do not sell personal data. We may share information as follows:

  • With the relevant Institute: Test-Taker performance data, recordings, and related information are shared with the Institute that enrolled the Test-Taker, for the purpose of test administration and reporting.
  • Service providers and subprocessors: We use third-party vendors to host infrastructure, store data, process payments, send communications, and (where enabled) provide proctoring, speech-scoring, or identity-verification technology. These providers are bound by contractual confidentiality and data protection obligations and may only use data to provide services to us.
  • Business transfers: In connection with a merger, acquisition, financing, or sale of assets, information may be transferred as part of that transaction, subject to standard confidentiality protections.
  • Legal requirements: We may disclose information if required by law, subpoena, or other legal process, or where we believe disclosure is necessary to protect our rights, the safety of our users, or the integrity of the Services.
  • With consent: We may share information with other third parties when an Institute or Test-Taker has given explicit consent to do so.

A current list of BandUltra's subprocessors is available upon request or, where applicable, at [insert subprocessor list URL].

8. Data Retention

We retain personal data for as long as necessary to provide the Services and fulfill the purposes described in this Policy, including:

  • Institute account data: for the duration of the customer relationship and for a reasonable period afterward for legal, billing, and audit purposes.
  • Test-Taker performance data and recordings: for the period specified in our agreement with the relevant Institute, or as configured by the Institute within the platform, after which data is deleted or anonymized unless a longer retention period is required by law.
  • Log and technical data: typically retained for a limited period for security and diagnostic purposes.

Institutes may request deletion of Test-Taker data in accordance with our DPA, subject to any legal retention obligations.

9. Cookies and Tracking Technologies

We use cookies and similar technologies to authenticate users, remember preferences, maintain session security (including during timed test-taking), and understand platform usage. Categories include:

  • Strictly necessary cookies — required for login, session management, and test-session security.
  • Functional cookies — remember settings and preferences.
  • Analytics cookies — help us understand usage patterns to improve the Services.

Where required by law, we will request consent before setting non-essential cookies. Users can typically manage cookie preferences through their browser settings; disabling certain cookies may affect the availability of some features, including the ability to take a test.

10. Data Security

We implement administrative, technical, and physical safeguards designed to protect personal data against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction, including encryption in transit, access controls, and regular security reviews. However, no method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Institutes and their personnel are responsible for maintaining the confidentiality of their account credentials and for promptly notifying us of any suspected unauthorized access.

11. International Data Transfers

BandUltra may process and store data in countries other than the country in which an Institute or Test-Taker is located. Where we transfer personal data internationally, we use appropriate safeguards required by applicable law, such as Standard Contractual Clauses, to ensure the data remains protected.

12. Your Rights

Depending on your location and applicable law, you may have rights regarding your personal data, including the right to:

  • Access, correct, or update your personal data.
  • Request deletion of your personal data.
  • Object to or restrict certain processing.
  • Request a copy of your data in a portable format.
  • Withdraw consent, where processing is based on consent.

Test-Takers should generally direct these requests to their Institute, which can coordinate with BandUltra as needed. Institute personnel may contact us directly using the details in Section 15. We will respond to verified requests within the timeframe required by applicable law.

13. Children's Privacy

The Services are designed for use by Institutes and their enrolled students, who may include minors preparing for standardized English-proficiency tests. Where Test-Takers are minors, we rely on the enrolling Institute (and, where applicable, parental/guardian consent obtained by the Institute) to authorize the collection and processing of the minor's data in accordance with applicable law. We do not knowingly collect personal data directly from children outside of this Institute-mediated context.

14. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices, technology, legal requirements, or for other operational reasons. We will post the updated Policy with a revised "Last Updated" date and, where required by law or where changes are material, provide additional notice to Institutes.
